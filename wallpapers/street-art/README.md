# Neon Mary Expressionistic / Graffiti / Street Wallpapers

A cohesive urban-art series derived from the supplied `mary.png` portrait. The
collection preserves the movie treatment system while reinterpreting each
variant as a hand-painted mural: gestural brushwork, spray overspray, drips,
scuffed wheatpaste, rough concrete, stencil-like planes, and expressive contour
marks.

## Collections

- `blade-runner` — neon-noir cyan, magenta, violet, mint, and sodium amber
- `crow` — charcoal, ash, weathered blue, mauve, olive, and blood red
- `amelie` — café brown, ochre, butter yellow, olive, teal, and poppy red
- `tron` — phosphor cyan, electric blue, ultraviolet, amber, and signal green
- `dark-city` — steel blue, clockwork violet, sodium amber, and muted crimson
- `fifth-element` — electric cyan, ultraviolet, solar amber, acid green, and coral
- `grand-budapest` — confectionery pink, aubergine, lacquer red, gold, and blue
- `evangelion` — Unit-01 purple, toxic green, signal orange, and warning red
- `matrix` — phosphor green, terminal lime, emerald, and red warning accents
- `solaris` — instrument amber, oxidized copper, umber, ocean teal, and ivory
- `suspiria` — blood red, cobalt blue, acid yellow, hot magenta, and black

Each collection contains the same eight wallpaper formats as the other gallery
series: `4k.png`, `wqhd.png`, `qhd.png`, `16-10.png`, `3-2.png`, `4-3.png`,
`1-1.png`, and `9-16.png`.

These are generated raster wallpaper assets and visual treatments. The original
portrait remains preserved separately as `wallpapers/original-mary-1254.png`.
