# Neon Mary Pop-Art Wallpapers

A cohesive vectorized screen-print pop-art collection derived from the supplied
`mary.png` portrait. Each treatment is rendered in eight wallpaper aspect
families and uses the repository's established accent-color families.

## Collections

- `blade-runner` — cyan, magenta, violet, mint, and amber neon noir
- `crow` — ash, weathered blue, mauve, olive, and blood red
- `amelie` — café brown, ochre, butter yellow, olive, teal, and poppy red
- `tron` — phosphor cyan, electric blue, violet, amber, and signal green
- `dark-city` — steel blue, clockwork violet, sodium amber, and muted crimson
- `fifth-element` — electric cyan, ultraviolet, solar amber, acid green, and coral
- `grand-budapest` — confectionery pink, aubergine, lacquer red, gold, and blue
- `evangelion` — Unit-01 purple, toxic green, signal orange, and warning red
- `suspiria` — blood red, cobalt blue, acid yellow, hot magenta, and black
- `akira` — Neo-Tokyo vermilion, electric blue, cyan, black, and warning orange
- `dune` — desert sand, spice orange, oxidized bronze, deep teal, and bone white
- `shining` — Overlook carpet red, icy blue, mustard gold, cream, and deep brown

Each collection contains:

- `4k.png` — 3840×2160
- `wqhd.png` — 2560×1440
- `qhd.png` — 1920×1080
- `16-10.png` — 2560×1600
- `3-2.png` — 2160×1440
- `4-3.png` — 2048×1536
- `1-1.png` — 2048×2048
- `9-16.png` — 1440×2560

These are generated visual examples/raster wallpaper assets. The source
portrait remains preserved separately as `wallpapers/original-mary-1254.png`.
